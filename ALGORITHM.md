#Algorithm

##Inputs

-Clean towels
-Soap/ Body Wash
-Shampoo
-Water supply
-Bathroom

## Process Steps

1.**Preparation:**
-Gather clean clothes and a towel.
-Enter the bathroom and close/ lock the door.
-Remove clothing.
2.**Water Temperature Adjustment:**
-Turn on the shower faucet.
-Test water temperature with hand.
-Adjust water temperature to taste.
3.**Washing:**
-Wash hair and body thoroughly.
-Apply soap and shampoo.
-Rinse till all soap is off the body.
4.**Completion:**
-Turn off the shower faucet.
-Grab the towel, dry the body.
-Tie the towel, look for clean clothes to wear.

##Output
-Body is clean and dry in clothes.
